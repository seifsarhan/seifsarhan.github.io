    About

    I design and build websites that feel fast — from pixel-perfect storefronts to Flask platforms with AI and payments built in. In Arabic and English.

    I'm Seifeldeen Mohamed Ibrahim Sarhan — Seif. A Business Informatics Systems graduate and the founder of The Social Club, a digital growth agency where I design, build and ship responsive, SEO-friendly sites for real estate, hospitality, travel, retail and pet care.

    I take an idea from a blank page to a working product — the interface people touch, and the database, payments and AI behind it. Most of my work switches cleanly between right-to-left Arabic and left-to-right English, and I sweat the details that make a site feel premium.
